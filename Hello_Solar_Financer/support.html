Frequently Asked Questions

Disbursements are processed automatically every business day at 3:00 PM PHT. Milestone 1 (equipment delivery) is credited within 24 hours of delivery proof, and Milestone 2 (grid tie-in) upon utility certificate verification.
Borrowers can use up to 40% of their monthly income to pay debt. If their debt is between 40% and 48%, they can still be approved by adding an employed co-signer or showing proof of 30%+ electric bill savings.
All solar panels, inverters, and battery storage are legally secured with a chattel mortgage registered with the government. The equipment remains secured collateral until the loan is fully paid.
Yes! Customers can pay off their loan early anytime with 0% penalty fees. The financer receives the remaining principal in full plus earned interest up to the date of settlement.
To reapply after a decline, borrowers simply submit: 3 months of recent pay slips or bank statements, an employed co-signer's government ID and utility bill, and an updated solar savings computation from the installer.
